Walking in Chilmark and Ridge

Chilmark has 18 different routes for you to explore the parish and the beautiful hamlet of Ridge. The routes have been split into three, paths in the village of Chilmark, routes around Ridge, and north up to the A303 & beyond.

Colours identify the different types of rights of way.
